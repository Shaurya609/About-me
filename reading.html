Edward and Alphonse Elric are the youngest alchemist but they committed a crime when they tried to revive their dead mother, thus making Edward loose his right arm and left leg replaced by prosthetic steel limbs (thus giving him the name "Full Metal Alchemist") and Alphonse loosing his body. Alphonse was saved when Edward transmuted his soul into a suit of armor. This story follow the adventures of the Elric brothers as they both try to restore their bodies uncovering the secrets of the army along the way.
